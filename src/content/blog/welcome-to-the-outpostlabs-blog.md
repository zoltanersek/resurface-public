We build small, focused apps for teams that live in Jira — starting with
[Resurface](/resurface/), a retro action item tracker that makes sure the things
you agree to in a retro actually get done.

This blog is where we'll share what we're working on and what we're learning:

- **Product notes** — new Resurface features, why we built them, and how to get the
  most out of them.
- **Jira tips** — practical, no-fluff ways to keep action items, retros, and
  follow-through from slipping through the cracks.
- **Behind the build** — the occasional look at how a tiny team ships apps on the
  Atlassian Forge platform.

No spam, no filler. If that sounds useful, you can also
[drop your email](/resurface/#updates) to hear about new posts and apps.

Thanks for reading — more soon.
